Questions & guarantee

Still have questions?

What if I don't have time to learn?

We provide free onboarding and user training, including a guided walkthrough of customers, quotations, orders, payments, and deliveries. You and your team won't need to learn everything on your own.

Can I keep using Messenger?

Yes. You can continue speaking with customers on Messenger and use Furniture Odyssey for organizing business records, quotations, orders, payments, and deliveries. Messenger is not directly integrated with the current system.

Will my existing data transfer?

Free assisted data migration is included. We'll help move supported existing records into the new system, subject to agreed data formats, volume, and verification. Handwritten and unusual historical records may need additional preparation.

What does the 30-day guarantee cover?

We aim to help customers transition to a more organized workflow within 30 days, supported by onboarding and training. The specific terms of any formal 30-day guarantee, including eligibility and remedies, will be communicated before purchase.
